# ArsaShop POS User Guide

## Purpose of This Guide

This user guide helps end-users (cashiers, store managers, owners) understand:
- How to navigate the system
- How to perform daily tasks
- How to troubleshoot common issues

**Written for non-technical users** - no programming knowledge required!

---

## A. Introduction

### What ArsaShop POS Is
ArsaShop POS is a complete Point of Sale system that helps you:
- Process sales quickly and efficiently
- Manage inventory automatically
- Track customer information
- Generate reports for business decisions
- Work offline when internet is unavailable

### Who Should Use It
- **Cashiers**: Process daily sales and customer transactions
- **Store Managers**: Manage inventory, staff, and daily operations
- **Business Owners**: Track sales, generate reports, and make business decisions

### Overview of Main Features
- **Point of Sale (POS)**: Fast checkout with barcode scanning
- **Product Management**: Add, edit, and organize products
- **Inventory Tracking**: Automatic stock updates and alerts
- **Sales Management**: Track all transactions and refunds
- **Customer Management**: Store customer information and purchase history
- **Reporting**: Daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports
- **Offline Mode**: Continue working without internet connection

## D. Step-by-Step Instructions for Key Features

### 1. Processing a Sale

**When to use it**: Every time a customer wants to buy products

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Click "New Sale"** from the dashboard or POS menu
2. **Add products** to the cart:
   - Scan barcode with scanner, OR
   - Search product by name, OR
   - Browse product categories
3. **Verify quantities** and prices shown in cart
4. **Select payment method** (cash, card, mobile payment)
5. **Enter payment amount** (for cash payments)
6. **Click "Complete Sale"**
7. **Print receipt** or send via email

**Tips**:
- Press F1 to quickly start a new sale
- Use barcode scanner for faster checkout
- Press Enter to confirm payment

**Screenshot**: *[Image: POS interface with products in cart]*

### 2. Adding Products

**When to use it**: When you have new products to sell

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Products** from the navigation menu
2. **Click "Add New Product"** button
3. **Fill in product information**:
   - Product name
   - Barcode/SKU
   - Price
   - Category
   - Initial stock quantity
4. **Upload product image** (optional but recommended)
5. **Click "Save Product"**

**Tips**:
- Use descriptive product names for easy searching
- Keep barcode numbers unique
- Set proper categories for better organization

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Add product form with fields highlighted]*

### 3. Managing Inventory

**When to use it**: When products arrive, when stock runs low, or for regular stock checks

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Inventory** from the navigation menu
2. **View current stock levels** for all products
3. **Update stock**:
   - Click "Add Stock" for new deliveries
   - Enter quantity received
   - Click "Update"
4. **Set low stock alerts**:
   - Click "Edit" on a product
   - Set minimum stock level
   - Save changes

**Tips**:
- Check inventory daily for popular items
- Set alerts 2-3 days before stock runs out
- Use barcode scanner for faster stock updates

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Inventory list with stock levels and add stock button]*

### 4. Creating Customer Accounts

**When to use it**: When new customers want to join your loyalty program or for repeat customers

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Customers** from the navigation menu
2. **Click "Add New Customer"**
3. **Enter customer information**:
   - Full name
   - Phone number
   - Email address
   - Address (optional)
4. **Click "Save Customer"**
5. **Link to sale** (optional):
   - During checkout, search customer name
   - Select customer from list
   - Sale will be linked to their account

**Tips**:
- Collect phone numbers for loyalty programs
- Use customer accounts for returns and exchanges
- Track purchase history for better service

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Customer creation form with required fields]*

### 5. Processing Refunds

**When to use it**: When customers return products or request refunds

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Sales** from the navigation menu
2. **Find the original sale**:
   - Search by date, customer, or receipt number
   - Click on the sale record
3. **Click "Process Refund"**
4. **Select items to refund**:
   - Check items being returned
   - Verify quantities
5. **Choose refund method** (cash, store credit, etc.)
6. **Enter refund amount**
7. **Click "Process Refund"**
8. **Print refund receipt**

**Tips**:
- Check store return policy before processing
- Require original receipt for all returns
- Document reason for return

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Refund processing screen with item selection]*

### 6. Daily Sales Closing & Reporting

**When to use it**: At the end of each business day

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Reports** from the navigation menu
2. **Select "Daily Sales Report"**
3. **Choose the date** (usually today)
4. **Review the report**:
   - Total sales amount
   - Number of transactions
   - Payment method breakdown
   - Top selling products
5. **Print or export report**:
   - Click "Print" for paper copy
   - Click "Export" for digital file
6. **Perform end-of-day closing**:
   - Count cash in drawer
   - Match with report totals
   - Record any discrepancies

**Tips**:
- Run reports at the same time each day
- Keep copies of daily reports for records
- Investigate any unusual transactions immediately

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Daily sales report with key metrics highlighted]*

### 7. Syncing to Cloud (Online Mode)

**When to use it**: When switching from offline to online mode, or to ensure data is backed up

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Connect to internet** via WiFi or ethernet
2. **Look for sync status** in top-right corner (green = online, red = offline)
3. **Click "Sync Now"** if not automatic
4. **Wait for sync completion**:
   - Green checkmark = successful
   - Red X = needs attention
   - Yellow circle = syncing in progress
5. **Review sync results** if any errors occurred
6. **Check pending items count** (shows number of items waiting to sync)

**Tips**:
- Sync automatically happens when internet is available
- Check sync status daily when using offline mode
- Contact support if sync fails repeatedly
- Large transactions may take longer to sync

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Sync status indicator with red arrow pointing to sync button]*

## F. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "Can't Log In"
**Problem**: Incorrect username or password
**Solution**:
1. Check your username and password spelling
2. Ensure Caps Lock is off
3. Click "Forgot Password" if available
4. Contact your system administrator

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Login screen with reset password option]*

#### "Printer Not Working"
**Problem**: Receipt printer not printing
**Solution**:
1. Check printer power and connection
2. Verify paper is loaded correctly
3. Restart the printer
4. Check printer settings in ArsaShop POS
5. Test with a different USB port

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Printer connection diagram]*

#### "Inventory Not Updating"
**Problem**: Stock levels not changing after sales
**Solution**:
1. Check if you're in offline mode
2. Sync data when internet is available
3. Verify product has stock tracking enabled
4. Check for error messages during sales

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Sync status indicator]*

#### "POS Not Syncing"
**Problem**: Data not syncing to cloud
**Solution**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify login credentials
3. Clear application cache
4. Restart ArsaShop POS
5. Contact support if problem continues

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Network status and sync button]*

#### "Barcode Scanner Not Working"
**Problem**: Scanner not reading barcodes
**Solution**:
1. Check scanner connection
2. Test scanner in notepad or text editor
3. Clean barcode labels
4. Verify scanner compatibility
5. Replace scanner batteries if wireless

## J. Offline Mode Complete Guide

### What Works Offline
✅ **Fully Functional**:
- Process all sales and transactions
- Add and edit products
- Manage inventory (add stock, adjust quantities)
- Create and manage customer accounts
- Generate reports (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Print receipts and documents
- Search products and customers
- Apply discounts and promotions

### What Does NOT Work Offline
❌ **Requires Internet**:
- Remote data backup to cloud
- Multi-store synchronization
- Email receipts and reports
- Software updates
- License validation (after initial activation)
- Online payment gateways (credit card processing)

### What Happens If Sync Fails
**Automatic Retry System**:
- ArsaShop POS will retry syncing every 5 minutes
- Failed sync attempts are logged for review
- Data remains safe on your local device
- You can continue working normally during sync failures

**Manual Sync Recovery**:
1. **Check internet connection** strength
2. **Restart ArsaShop POS** application
3. **Click \"Force Sync\"** in settings
4. **Review error messages** in sync log
5. **Export backup** if sync continues failing

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Sync failure notification with retry button highlighted]*

### Best Practices for Offline-Only Stores
**Daily Operations**:
- **Start each day**: Check pending sync count
- **During day**: Monitor sync status indicator
- **End of day**: Ensure all data is synced if possible
- **Weekly**: Export manual backups to USB drive

**Data Safety**:
- **Enable automatic local backups** in settings
- **Keep backup files** on separate storage device
- **Test restore process** monthly
- **Document any sync issues** with dates and times

**Network Planning**:
- **Consider mobile hotspot** as backup internet
- **Schedule sync times** during off-peak hours
- **Monitor data usage** if using limited internet plans
- **Have IT support contact** ready for network issues

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Offline mode settings with backup options highlighted]*

## K. Advanced Sales Process & Edge Cases

### Split Payments
**When to use it**: Customer wants to pay with multiple methods (cash + card, multiple cards, etc.)

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Complete the sale** as normal with all products
2. **Click \"Split Payment\"** instead of single payment method
3. **Enter first payment amount**:
   - Select payment method (cash, card, mobile)
   - Enter amount for this payment
   - Click \"Add Payment\"
4. **Add additional payments**:
   - Repeat for each payment method
   - See remaining balance automatically calculated
5. **Complete sale** when total is fully paid
6. **Print receipt** showing all payment methods

**Example**: $100 purchase
- Payment 1: $60 cash
- Payment 2: $40 credit card

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Split payment screen with multiple payment methods highlighted]*

### Partial Payments & Layaways
**When to use it**: Customer pays deposit now, pays rest later

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Add products** to cart as normal
2. **Click \"Layaway\"** or \"Partial Payment\" button
3. **Enter deposit amount** customer is paying today
4. **Set payment schedule**:
   - Choose final payment date
   - Set reminder frequency
   - Add customer notes if needed
5. **Print layaway receipt** with payment schedule
6. **Track layaways** in Sales → Layaways section

**Tips**:
- Require minimum 20% deposit for layaways
- Set clear expiration dates (usually 30-90 days)
- Get customer contact information for reminders

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Layaway setup screen with deposit and schedule fields]*

### Applying Discounts During Sale
**When to use it**: Customer has discount code or you want to apply special pricing

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Add products** to cart
2. **Click \"Add Discount\"** button
3. **Choose discount type**:
   - **Percentage**: Enter % off (10%, 20%, etc.)
   - **Amount**: Enter dollar amount off ($5, $10, etc.)
   - **Fixed Price**: Set specific price for item
4. **Apply discount** to:
   - **Single item**: Click product, then apply discount
   - **Entire cart**: Apply to subtotal
5. **Enter discount code** if customer has one
6. **Verify new total** and complete sale

**Discount Rules**:
- Only one discount per item (unless manager override)
- Cannot combine percentage and amount discounts
- Some items may be excluded from discounts

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Discount application screen with red arrows pointing to discount options]*

### Voiding a Sale
**When to use it**: Mistake was made during sale, customer changed mind, or system error

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Sales** from navigation menu
2. **Find the sale** to void (today's sales shown by default)
3. **Click \"Void Sale\"** button
4. **Select reason for void**:
   - Customer changed mind
   - System error
   - Wrong items scanned
   - Other (with explanation)
5. **Enter manager password** (required for security)
6. **Confirm void action**
7. **Print void receipt** for records

**Important Rules**:
- Only today's sales can be voided
- Previous days require manager approval and refund process
- Voided sales cannot be restored
- All voids are logged in audit trail

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Void sale confirmation screen with reason selection highlighted]*

### Price Overrides
**When to use it**: Manager needs to change price for special circumstances

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Add product** to cart
2. **Click product** to highlight it
3. **Click \"Edit Price\"** button
4. **Enter new price**:
   - Type new price directly
   - Or enter percentage discount
5. **Enter manager password** for authorization
6. **Add override reason** (required):
   - Price match
   - Damaged packaging
   - Customer discount
   - Employee error
7. **Continue with sale** normally

**Security**:
- Requires manager-level permissions
- All overrides logged with user ID
- Daily report shows all price overrides

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Price override screen with password field highlighted]*

## L. Data Recovery & Backup Complete Guide

### Automatic Backup Schedule
**Default Backup Times**:
- **Every 4 hours**: Incremental backup (changes only)
- **Daily at 2 AM**: Full backup of all data
- **Weekly on Sunday**: Archive backup to external storage
- **Monthly**: Long-term archive backup

**Backup Locations**:
- **Primary**: `C:\ProgramData\ArsaShopPOS\Backups\`
- **Secondary**: USB drive if connected
- **Cloud**: When internet is available (if enabled)

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Backup schedule settings with times highlighted]*

### Manual Export/Import
**Exporting Data**:
1. **Go to Settings → Data Management**
2. **Click \"Export Data\"**
3. **Choose export type**:
   - **Sales**: All transactions for date range
   - **Products**: Complete product catalog
   - **Customers**: Customer database
   - **Complete Backup**: Everything
4. **Select date range** (if applicable)
5. **Choose export format**:
   - **CSV**: For Excel/spreadsheet use
   - **JSON**: For technical use
   - **ArsaShop Format**: For restore operations
6. **Select destination**:
   - Local computer
   - USB drive
   - Network location
7. **Click \"Start Export\"**

**Importing Data**:
1. **Go to Settings → Data Management**
2. **Click \"Import Data\"**
3. **Select file to import**
4. **Choose import options**:
   - **Merge with existing**: Add to current data
   - **Replace existing**: Delete current, import new
   - **Preview first**: See what will be imported
5. **Map fields** if using CSV files
6. **Click \"Import Data\"**
7. **Review import results**

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Data export screen with format options highlighted]*

### Restore from Backup Procedure
**Emergency Restore** (when system fails):
1. **Install fresh copy** of ArsaShop POS on new computer
2. **Launch application** and go through initial setup
3. **Skip license activation** if internet unavailable
4. **Go to Settings → Restore Backup**
5. **Select backup file** from USB drive or backup location
6. **Choose restore type**:
   - **Complete Restore**: Replace everything
   - **Selective Restore**: Choose what to restore
7. **Enter restore password** (if backup was encrypted)
8. **Confirm restore action**
9. **Wait for restore completion**
10. **Verify data integrity** by checking recent sales

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Restore backup screen with file selection highlighted]*

### Backup File Locations
**Windows**:
- **Local Backups**: `C:\ProgramData\ArsaShopPOS\Backups\`
- **User Backups**: `C:\Users\[Username]\Documents\ArsaShopPOS\Backups\`
- **Temporary**: `C:\Users\[Username]\AppData\Local\ArsaShopPOS\Temp\`

**Mac**:
- **Local Backups**: `/Library/Application Support/ArsaShopPOS/Backups/`
- **User Backups**: `~/Documents/ArsaShopPOS/Backups/`

**Linux**:
- **Local Backups**: `/var/lib/ArsaShop-pos/backups/`
- **User Backups**: `~/Documents/ArsaShopPOS/Backups/`

**Backup File Naming Convention**:
- `ArsaShop_backup_YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS.arsa`
- `ArsaShop_incremental_YYYY-MM-DD.arsa`
- `ArsaShop_manual_[Description]_YYYY-MM-DD.arsa`

**Tips**:
- Keep at least 30 days of backup history
- Store important backups off-site (cloud, USB drive)
- Test restore process monthly
- Label backup drives clearly with dates

### Data Sync Between Stores
**Automatic Sync Features**:
- **Product Catalog**: Changes sync to all stores immediately
- **Customer Database**: Shared across all stores
- **Inventory Levels**: Real-time updates between stores
- **Sales Reports**: Consolidated reporting available to managers

**Manual Sync Options**:
- **Force Sync Now**: Immediate sync of all pending changes
- **Sync Selected Data**: Choose specific data types to sync
- **Schedule Sync**: Set specific times for large data transfers

**Sync Priority Rules**:
1. **Sales transactions**: Highest priority (sync immediately)
2. **Inventory changes**: High priority (sync within 1 minute)
3. **Product updates**: Medium priority (sync within 5 minutes)
4. **Customer updates**: Low priority (sync within 15 minutes)

### Inventory Transfers Between Stores
**When to use it**: Moving stock from one store to another

**Step-by-step instructions**:
1. **Go to Inventory → Store Transfers**
2. **Click \"New Transfer\"**
3. **Select source store** (where stock is coming from)
4. **Select destination store** (where stock is going)
5. **Add products to transfer**:
   - Search product name or scan barcode
   - Enter quantity to transfer
   - Verify source store has sufficient stock
6. **Set transfer priority**:
   - **Urgent**: Same-day delivery required
   - **Normal**: Within 3 days
   - **Low**: Within 7 days
7. **Add notes** (transfer reason, special instructions)
8. **Click \"Create Transfer\"**
9. **Print transfer form** for shipping/receiving

**Receiving Store Process**:
1. **Go to Inventory → Pending Transfers**
2. **Select transfer** to receive
3. **Verify received quantities** against transfer form
4. **Update stock levels** automatically
5. **Confirm receipt** to complete transfer

**Impact on Reports**:
- **Source Store**: Stock reduced, transfer cost recorded
- **Destination Store**: Stock increased, transfer cost recorded
- **Consolidated Report**: No net change in total inventory value

## N. Security & User Permissions Guide

### Password Policy Requirements
**Minimum Requirements**:
- **Length**: At least 8 characters
- **Complexity**: Must include uppercase, lowercase, and numbers
- **Expiration**: Passwords expire every 90 days
- **History**: Cannot reuse last 5 passwords
- **Lockout**: Account locked after 5 failed attempts (30 minutes)

**Recommended Practices**:
- **Use passphrases**: "CorrectHorseBatteryStaple" is better than "P@ssw0rd!"
- **Avoid common words**: Don't use "password", "admin", "123456"
- **Store securely**: Don't write passwords on sticky notes
- **Change immediately** if compromised

**Password Reset Process**:
1. **User clicks \"Forgot Password\"** on login screen
2. **Enter email address** associated with account
3. **Check email** for reset link (valid for 2 hours)
4. **Create new password** meeting requirements
5. **Confirm new password** by entering again
6. **Login with new password**

**Screenshot**: *[Image: Password requirements screen with complexity indicators highlighted]*

## H. Glossary

**POS**: Point of Sale - the system where you process customer purchases

**SKU**: Stock Keeping Unit - a unique code for each product

**Stock**: The quantity of products available for sale

**Gross Sales**: Total sales amount before any deductions

**Net Sales**: Total sales after returns and discounts

**Inventory**: All products you have available for sale

**Barcode**: The pattern of lines that scanners read to identify products

**Transaction**: One complete sale or refund

**Receipt**: The proof of purchase given to customers

**Cloud Sync**: Copying your data to internet servers for backup

**Offline Mode**: Working without internet connection
